# AyahSpring Content Licence and Contributor Terms

**Status:** draft, adopted 2026-10-15 as decision D6.
**Not legal advice.** Have this reviewed by a lawyer qualified in your operating
jurisdiction before the first teacher accepts it. The structure below is
deliberately conventional so review is cheap.

Presented as a versioned click-through at first upload. Store the version
number and acceptance timestamp against the contributor.

---

## 1. Who owns the content

You keep ownership and copyright in everything you upload. AyahSpring does not
take ownership of your material.

## 2. What you grant us

You grant AyahSpring a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, sublicensable
licence to:

- host, store, back up and archive your content
- transcode, compress, caption, thumbnail and otherwise prepare it for delivery
- display, stream and distribute it to enrolled learners on web and mobile
- include it in search results, previews and platform promotion
- retain archival copies, including after you stop publishing

## 3. Learners who already enrolled

If you unpublish a course, **new** enrolments stop. Learners who already
enrolled keep access for as long as their enrolment entitles them to it, and
certificates already issued remain valid.

This is not negotiable — a certificate we issued refers to content that must
remain verifiable.

## 4. What we will not do

We will not create editorial derivatives of your content — re-cutting,
re-voicing, translating, abridging or re-attributing — without your separate
written consent. Technical preparation under section 2 is not an editorial
derivative.

## 5. What you warrant

You warrant that:

- you created the content, or hold the rights to upload it
- any quoted or referenced third-party material is used lawfully, and you can
  identify the source and edition on request
- the content does not infringe anyone's rights or defame anyone
- you are qualified to teach the subject matter you submit

## 6. Review before publication

Content is reviewed before publication. Review is about scholarly accuracy and
platform standards. **Review does not transfer ownership and is not an
endorsement of your qualifications.**

## 7. Removal

You may request removal at any time. We will stop new distribution promptly.
We may retain archival and legally required copies, and copies needed to serve
learners under section 3.

We may remove content that breaches these terms, fails review, or is suspended
under the platform's governance process.

## 8. Earnings

Where a course generates revenue, earnings are calculated and paid under the
separate instructor earnings terms in force at the time. Those terms are not
part of this licence.

## 9. Changes

We may update these terms. Material changes require notice and a fresh
acceptance. Continued publishing after acceptance of a new version constitutes
acceptance.
